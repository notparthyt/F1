        Keyboard pedals ramp in ~0.17 s and release in 0.1 s; keyboard steering moves toward lock at 3/s and returns at 5/s. Gamepad (standard layout): triggers are analog pedals, left stick steers with a deadzone and progressive curve. Steering authority falls with speed for both.

        Tire force / peak grip

        FL / FR / RL / RR · normal load in kN.
        Bars: force / peak capacity, not remaining grip. SLIDE: combined demand beyond the illustrative force peak; force falls toward 75% of peak. Slip: signed longitudinal slip; + is wheelspin when moving forward. LOCK: stopped wheel above 1 m/s. α: force-producing (relaxed) / kinematic slip angle; the carcass catches up over 0.45 m of travel. Temp: tread / carcass °C · thermal grip (blue cold, green near the 100 °C optimum, red hot); sliding heats the tread, airflow cools it. Wear: tread used · wear grip; sliding energy wears the tread (faster above the optimum), grip fades then falls off a cliff past 75% wear (amber above 37.5%, red above 75%); qualifying uses fresh Soft tires with no grip loss; fresh Softs wear and lose grip once the main race starts. Sim handling has no ABS or traction control; Arcade handling adds grip, stronger slide recovery, and reduces braking in turns.

        On foot: spawn beside your car. Click the viewport to capture the mouse; use W A S D to walk, double-tap W to run, press Space to jump. Aim at your car and press L to enter. Press E while standing still to cycle emotes. Q1, Q2, and Q3 run before the race, and qualifying determines the grid. You can exit with L after finishing a race (or completing a lap in practice).

        Esc Pause · R Reset · V Chase / overview · H Hide / show HUD · C Cycle cameras · U 360 Look; drag to orbit and zoom without passing through the car or track boundary
        B Request tyres: Soft → Medium → Hard → cancel. Auto-pit assistance, when enabled, takes control near pit entry and returns it after pit exit. Otherwise, drive into the pit lane and stop in your box.
        On the start lights, 1–5 (gamepad LB / RB) changes grid slot. Penalties appear as notices and affect final classification; running order is not immediately changed.
        Gamepad: RT throttle · LT brake · left stick steer · RB / LB shift · Y pause / resume · Start HUD · B camera · X fullscreen · A units · Back reset

        Illustrative, uncalibrated dynamics. Body roll, pitch and aero squat are damped spring modes for display; wheel loads are still quasi-static. Surface grip (assumed): kerb 90%, runoff 70%, grass 35%. Walls are rigid: the body footprint rebounds with 25% restitution and scrubs with friction 0.5; no damage model. Tires start at 70 °C (blanket temperature) and have a two-node thermal model and tread wear; no pressure or camber yet. Compounds (assumed): soft +1.5% grip and 2× wear, hard −1.5% grip and 0.55× wear relative to the medium; a pit stop fits a new set at blanket temperature after a 2.1–3.2 s service. Sound is synthesized from engine rpm, throttle, turbo spool, tire sliding power and speed (V6 firing frequency; no recorded samples). This is a handling testbed, not a validated F1 simulator.
